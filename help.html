Other free care options

Mental health crisis help

If you or someone you know is thinking about suicide, or is in a mental health or substance use crisis, you can get help right now. It's free, confidential, and open 24 hours a day.

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text) 988
  • NH Rapid Response (call or text) 1-833-710-6477

NH Rapid Response can send a crisis team to you anywhere in New Hampshire. You can also chat online at NH988.com. Veterans can call 988 and press 1.

Talk to someone

For help finding free or low-cost care, dial 2-1-1 or visit 211nh.org. It's free and confidential.

Get health coverage

NH Medicaid offers free or low-cost health coverage to people with lower incomes, and you can apply at any time of year. Apply online at NH EASY or call 1-844-275-3447.

Get a ride to the doctor

If you have NH Medicaid, your plan may give you a free ride to covered medical appointments. Call your plan at least 2 business days before your appointment. Your plan's name is on your member ID card.

  • AmeriHealth Caritas New Hampshire 1-833-301-2264
  • NH Healthy Families 1-888-597-1192
  • WellSense Health Plan 1-844-909-7433

If a friend or family member drives you, your plan may pay them back for mileage. Call your plan before the trip to set it up.

Not sure which plan you have, or don't have Medicaid? Call NH Medicaid at 1-844-275-3447, or dial 2-1-1 to find ride programs near you.

Save on prescriptions

  • Community health centers: many offer lower-cost medicines to their patients through a federal program called 340B. Ask the health center about it.
  • Medicare: if your income and savings are limited, Extra Help can lower your prescription costs. Apply at SSA.gov or call 1-800-772-1213 (TTY 1-800-325-0778).
  • Everyone: ask your doctor or pharmacist if a generic medicine is available. Generics usually cost less.
