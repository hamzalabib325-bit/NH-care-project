Other free care options

Talk to someone

For help finding free or low-cost care, dial 2-1-1 or visit 211nh.org. It's free and confidential.

Get health coverage

NH Medicaid offers free or low-cost health coverage to people with lower incomes, and you can apply at any time of year. Apply online at NH EASY or call 1-844-275-3447.
