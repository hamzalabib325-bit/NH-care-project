Which kind of place is right for me?

  • Free clinic

    Care at no cost, usually for people without insurance. Many need an appointment, so call first.

  • Community health center

    Regular doctor visits, checkups, and more. Fees go down if your income is lower, and they accept Medicaid and Medicare.

  • Urgent care

    Walk-in care for problems that can't wait but aren't emergencies, like a sprain, the flu, or a small cut. Usually costs more without insurance.

How it works

  1. Search with your location, ZIP code, or town.
  2. Answer a few quick questions about insurance and income to see which clinics fit your budget.
  3. Call or get directions straight from the app.

Your information stays with you

Your location and answers are only used on your own device to find nearby clinics. They are never saved or sent anywhere.

This app is a guide, not medical advice. Call ahead to confirm hours and costs.
