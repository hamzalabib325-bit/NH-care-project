About NH Care Finder

Many people put off seeing a doctor because they're worried about the cost. NH Care Finder helps people in New Hampshire find the closest free and low-cost clinics and urgent care centers, and understand what they're likely to pay before they go.

Built for the 2026 Congressional App Challenge.

Your privacy

  • Your location is only used on your device to measure distances. It is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your answers on the cost page never leave your device and are gone when you close the page.
  • There are no accounts, cookies, or tracking.
  • If you pick a language, that choice is remembered on your device so every page uses it.

Where the information comes from

  • Health centers: U.S. Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA), Health Center Service Delivery and Look-Alike Sites.
  • Urgent care centers: the national provider registry (NPPES NPI Registry) and providers' own websites.
  • Free clinics and mobile programs: the providers' own websites.
  • Town and ZIP code locations: U.S. Census Bureau 2024 Gazetteer files.
  • Cost rules: 2026 HHS Poverty Guidelines, HRSA health center sliding-fee requirements, and NH Department of Health and Human Services Medicaid rules.
  • Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, shown with Leaflet.

Please call ahead

Hours, prices, and services change. This app is a guide, not medical advice or a price quote. Always call a clinic to confirm before you go. In an emergency, call 911.
